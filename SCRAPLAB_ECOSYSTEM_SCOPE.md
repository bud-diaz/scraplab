# SCRAPLAB ECOSYSTEM SCOPE
## Media-Backed Product Ecosystem MVP

Version: 1.0
Status: Strategic Venture Scope

---

# Executive Summary

ScrapLab is a family creativity brand built around transforming everyday household materials into imaginative, kid-friendly projects.

The ecosystem combines:

- a utility-first software product
- family-friendly media content
- short-form social growth
- educational expansion opportunities
- future physical product extensions

Initial wedge:
A web application that helps parents identify available household materials and receive safe, age-appropriate build ideas.

Growth engine:
Authentic creator-led content showing those builds being made in real life.

Core thesis:
Software solves utility.
Media creates emotional attachment.
Together they create defensibility.

---

# Vision

Build the go-to creativity ecosystem for families who want imaginative play without constant spending on toys, kits, or structured entertainment.

ScrapLab should feel like:

part utility app  
part family creativity show  
part maker challenge brand  
part educational platform

Long-term aspiration:
Become a recognizable family creativity IP spanning software, content, education, and physical experiences.

---

# Problem Statement

Modern families face several friction points:

## Parent Problems
- decision fatigue
- expensive toy/craft spending
- clutter from saved "future project" materials
- difficulty finding quick engaging activities
- too much passive screen entertainment
- overwhelming low-quality Pinterest-style discovery

## Child Problems
- boredom
- unstructured creative energy
- limited agency in project ideation
- short attention spans requiring immediate payoff

## Market Gap
Existing craft ecosystems are fragmented:
- content-heavy blogs
- supply-first kits
- generic activity apps

Very few products are inventory-first, child-safe, and media-driven.

# Core Strategic Thesis

ScrapLab wins through a flywheel.

MEDIA → PRODUCT → COMMUNITY → RETENTION

Example loop:
1. Family sees ScrapLab YouTube short
2. Downloads / uses ScrapLab app
